# CRM Peyvand v1.1.0

The **payments** release. Money received from a customer is now a first-class
record instead of a checkbox on the invoice, and the two-unit problem that had
been quietly doubling every figure on screen is fixed.

** upgrading:** download `CRMPeyvand.msi` and run it over your existing install.
Your data migrates itself — on SQL Server the widened money columns alter in
place at startup, and an existing SQLite file is left exactly as it is.

---

## 💰 Receivables & payments

Payments are now the authoritative record of what a customer owes. The invoice's
paid state is derived from them, so the two can no longer disagree.

- **Payment entity** — amount, Payment Instrument (cash, card, transfer, cheque,
  other), an optional reference number, who took it, and when. Persisted on both
  SQLite and SQL Server.
- **Derived balances** — every Invoice and every Customer now carries an
  Outstanding Balance computed from its payments.
- **A payment can never exceed what is owed.** Validated before it is recorded;
  overpayment is refused rather than held as credit.
- **Void, never amend** — a payment may already have a printed receipt in the
  customer's hand, so a correction is a void plus a fresh payment rather than an
  edit.
- **New «دریافت‌ها» ledger screen** — every payment, searchable by invoice number
  or customer name. Reachable from the sidebar, from shortcut `V`, or by
  right-clicking any unpaid invoice and choosing «ثبت وصولی».
- **Cashier-safe access** — Payments is its own permission Section, so a cashier
  can take money without being able to issue invoices, and therefore without
  being able to reduce stock.
- **Settle at the counter in one click** — the invoice screen's «وضعیت پرداخت»
  checkbox now hands the invoice a real payment, written in the same transaction
  that writes the invoice, so an invoice and its receipt cannot come apart.
- **Customer grid** gains a balance column, and the **dashboard** gains a debtor
  count.

### 🧾 New printed documents

- **رسید دریافت (Payment Receipt)** — one per payment, carrying the invoice
  total, what that receipt paid, what is left, the Payment Instrument and who
  took it.
- **مانده حساب (Customer Balance Statement)** — every customer who still owes
  something, with their invoice count, total billed, total received and balance.
  Wired into the reports window.
- The invoice document and the invoice details screen gain their paid and balance
  lines.

## 💱 The two currencies, finally reconciled

This was a real bug, not a cosmetic one: **there was no conversion anywhere.** A
single raw number was stored and labelled two different ways — «تومان» on the
price, discount and payment fields, «ریال» in every report and in three hardcoded
column headers. A price stored at 300,000 read as *300,000 تومان* on screen and
printed as *300,000 ریال* on the receipt — a tenfold disagreement about the same
money.

Now:

- **Rial is what is stored, and what is printed.** Every printed document says
  ریال and shows the stored figure unaltered, because a document that leaves the
  building is read by an accountant.
- **Toman is what the screens show and you type.** Ten Toman make a Rial, so a
  price on screen is a tenth of the number in the database. The same payment
  appears as `15,000 تومان` in the ledger grid and `150,000 ریال` on its receipt.
  That is correct, not a discrepancy.
- **One conversion, in one place** — `Money.ToToman` / `Money.FromToman`, applied
  by `Money.Display` and `MoneyConverter`. Nothing is scaled by writing `/10` at a
  call site, so no screen can end up printing a stored figure under a Toman label.
- **Display floors, saving is exact.** Rial do not divide into Toman evenly
  (1,555 Rial is 155.5 Toman), so a shown price is floored and is never worth more
  than the money behind it. Multiplying on the way in loses nothing.
- Stock counts are left alone — a quantity is not money and is not scaled.

## 🐛 Fixes

- **Price fields no longer cap out at ~21 billion Rial.** Prices were parsed into
  an `int`, so anything above 2,147,483,647 came back empty. A price of
  30,000,000,000 is a plain figure for this business and now works end to end.
- **A full form is no longer reported as empty.** That same ceiling returned the
  same `null` an empty field does, so editing a product with every field filled in
  was refused with «لطفاً تمام فیلدها را پر کنید». Blank, non-numeric and
  out-of-range now get distinct messages — an oversized amount says so by name.
- **Money columns widened** from `DECIMAL(18,2)` to `DECIMAL(28,2)`, leaving
  storage six digits wider than the widest figure a field can ask it to hold, so
  the schema can never be the thing that surprises you. Runs automatically on
  SQL Server at startup; SQLite needs nothing.
- **Saving a percentage discount code no longer throws.** The amount field is
  blank for a percentage code by design, but the handler read it anyway and blew
  up with the global error box instead of saving.
- **The invoice number field no longer queries the database on every keystroke.**
  Typing "12" fired two lookups and popped two "not found" dialogs over the
  number being typed. It now resolves on `LostFocus` or `Enter` — the idiom the
  invoice screen already used for its discount code — and reports the outcome in
  the label beside the field rather than a modal.
- Sidebar menu moved up one row, closing the gap under the username.
- Dashboard stat strip right-aligned so figures fill from the right inward,
  matching the panel below it; box margins lined up with their neighbours.
- Payments screen rebuilt: each label now sits on the same line as its own input,
  the buttons are no longer clipped by their row, and the Payment Instrument
  dropdown no longer collapses and clips itself.

## 📦 Installer

- **The version now actually reaches the MSI.** The app carried its own
  `<Version>1.0.0</Version>` with nothing tying it to the installer's
  `ProductVersion`, so following the documented release steps would have published
  a package labelled 1.1.0 wrapping an executable that reported 1.0.0 to Windows.
  `build-installer.ps1` also defaulted to 1.0.0 on a bare invocation.
- **Re-running a release tag replaces the MSI asset** instead of failing on a
  second release, and `--latest` is set at create time.

## 📚 Documentation

- **README corrected** — it still credited Stimulsoft for invoice printing, which
  ADR-0007 retired in favour of QuestPDF, and listed a package that is not a
  dependency. The stale SQL Server known issue is removed rather than reworded:
  it was fixed some time ago and the note outlived the fix.
- **New ADR-0008 — dual-database mode**, recording the SQLite/SQL Server choice and
  the three consequences that bite later: the two connections are deliberately
  returned in different open/closed states, the `providerName` token must be
  stripped before either connection type will accept the string, and a shared
  machine user who cannot write to ProgramData silently gets their own database.
- **New ADR-0009 — payments are authoritative over the checkout flag**, with the
  reasoning behind voiding rather than amending.
- `CONTEXT.md` and the feature list brought in line with the payments work,
  including the two-unit rule in both English and Persian.

## ✅ Verification

346 tests, all passing (243 at v1.0.0). New coverage includes the payment
ledger against a real database file — recording, voiding and the derived checkout
flag — the settlement policy, the receipt and statement report models, the
dashboard and grid queries on both providers, the money conversions and field
ranges, and a guard that reads the declared column precision out of the schema so
the column and the field ceiling cannot drift apart unnoticed.

### Known issues

- The MSI is **unsigned** — no signing certificate is configured, so SmartScreen
  will warn on first run.
- Existing **SQLite** databases keep the `DECIMAL(18,2)` columns they were
  created with. This costs nothing: SQLite gives `DECIMAL` a NUMERIC affinity and
  enforces neither precision nor scale. Only newly created SQLite files pick up
  the widened declaration.
